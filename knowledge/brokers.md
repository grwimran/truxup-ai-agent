# Truxup — Brokers

## Overview

Truxup supports brokers with tools for managing broker transportation workflows from load management through tracking, billing, settlements, and reporting.

## Broker Capabilities

Truxup supports broker workflows including:

* Load management
* Carrier matching
* Load posting
* Scheduling
* Carrier management
* Load visibility
* Tracking
* Communication
* Document management
* Compliance
* Billing
* Settlements
* Analytics and reporting

## Load Management

Brokers can manage transportation loads within Truxup.

The platform supports workflows for organizing loads, scheduling transportation, tracking progress, and managing related documents.

## Carrier Matching

Truxup supports carrier matching within broker workflows.

This helps broker teams manage the process of connecting loads with carriers.

## Load Posting

Truxup supports load posting as part of broker transportation workflows.

## Scheduling

Truxup provides scheduling capabilities for managing transportation activities.

The Dynamic Scheduler supports load assignments, unassigned driver visibility, HOS-related considerations, and scheduling.

## Carrier Management

Truxup provides tools for managing carriers within broker workflows.

Broker teams can manage carrier-related transportation activities from a centralized platform.

## Load Visibility

Truxup provides centralized load visibility and status information.

This allows broker teams to maintain visibility into transportation activity.

## Tracking

Truxup supports real-time tracking and updates.

The platform also provides Live Map visibility.

## Communication

Truxup supports communication within transportation workflows, helping teams coordinate transportation activities.

## Documents and Compliance

Truxup supports digital document management, imaging, document scanning, and compliance-related workflows.

Proof of delivery is also supported.

## Billing and Settlements

Truxup provides billing and invoicing capabilities.

The platform also supports settlement workflows.

## Reporting and Analytics

Truxup provides reporting and analytics capabilities for transportation operations.

## Broker Use Case

A broker can use Truxup to manage workflows such as:

Load Management → Carrier Matching → Scheduling → Tracking → Delivery & Documents → Billing & Settlements → Reporting
