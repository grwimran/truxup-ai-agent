# Truxup — Carriers

## Overview

Truxup supports transportation carriers with tools for managing their transportation operations from load entry through reporting.

## Carrier Capabilities

Truxup supports carrier workflows including:

* Load management
* Dispatch
* Route planning
* Real-time tracking
* Driver management
* Driver communication
* Document management
* Billing and invoicing
* Driver settlements
* Reporting and analytics
* Compliance
* Proof of delivery
* Driver mobile tools

## Load Management

Carriers can manage transportation loads within Truxup.

The platform supports the transportation workflow from load entry through dispatch, tracking, delivery, invoicing, settlements, and reporting.

## Dispatch

Truxup provides dispatch tools for managing loads and driver assignments.

Dispatchers can use scheduling and load visibility tools to coordinate transportation operations.

## Dynamic Scheduler

The Dynamic Scheduler supports:

* Load assignments
* Unassigned driver visibility
* HOS-related considerations
* Scheduling
* Drag-and-drop scheduling workflow

This helps dispatch teams organize loads and driver assignments.

## Real-Time Tracking

Truxup provides real-time tracking and updates so transportation teams can maintain visibility into loads and movements.

The platform also provides Live Map visibility.

## Driver Mobile Tools

Truxup provides driver mobile tools through Truxup Connect / its driver mobile app.

Driver workflows include:

* Viewing assigned loads
* Navigation
* Communication
* Proof of delivery

## Documents and Proof of Delivery

Truxup supports digital document management, imaging, document scanning, and proof-of-delivery workflows.

## Billing and Invoicing

Truxup provides billing and invoicing capabilities as part of the carrier transportation workflow.

## Driver Settlements

Truxup supports driver settlements and driver pay workflows.

## Reporting and Analytics

Truxup provides reporting and analytics capabilities to help transportation businesses review their operations.

## Compliance

Truxup supports compliance-related workflows.

The Dynamic Scheduler includes HOS-related considerations.

## Carrier Use Case

A carrier can use Truxup to manage a transportation workflow such as:

Load Entry → Dispatch & Track → Delivery & Invoicing → Settlements → Reporting
