# 1. Product

## What is TruxUp?

TruxUp is an AI-powered, cloud-based Transportation Management System (TMS) designed for carriers, brokers, and hybrid logistics companies.

## Is TruxUp cloud-based?

Yes. TruxUp is a cloud-based Transportation Management System.

## Who is TruxUp designed for?

TruxUp is designed for:

* Carriers
* Brokers
* Hybrid logistics companies

## What type of transportation operations can TruxUp support?

TruxUp supports transportation workflows including:

* Load management
* Dispatch
* Scheduling
* Driver management
* Tracking
* Transportation visibility
* Documents
* Billing
* Invoicing
* Settlements
* Reporting
* Analytics
* Communication

The exact workflows available to a customer may depend on the customer's setup and requirements.
