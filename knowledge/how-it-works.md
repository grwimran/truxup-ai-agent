# Truxup — How It Works

## Overview

Truxup supports a transportation workflow from load entry through reporting.

The overall workflow is:

**Load Entry → Dispatch & Track → Delivery & Invoicing → Settlements → Reporting**

## Step 1 — Load Entry

Transportation loads are entered and managed in Truxup.

Load management provides the starting point for the transportation workflow.

## Step 2 — Dispatch & Track

After a load is entered, transportation teams can dispatch and track it.

Truxup provides capabilities including:

* Dispatch
* Driver assignment
* Scheduling
* Dynamic Scheduler
* Real-time tracking
* Live Map
* Communication
* Load visibility

The Dynamic Scheduler supports load assignments, unassigned driver visibility, HOS-related considerations, and scheduling.

## Step 3 — Delivery & Invoicing

During and after transportation, Truxup supports delivery and financial workflows.

These include:

* Proof of delivery
* Digital document management
* Imaging
* Document scanning
* Billing
* Invoicing

## Step 4 — Settlements

Truxup supports driver settlements and driver pay workflows.

This allows settlements to be handled as part of the overall transportation workflow.

## Step 5 — Reporting

Truxup provides reporting and analytics capabilities.

Transportation businesses can use reporting to review their operations and transportation activity.

## Driver Workflow

Truxup provides driver mobile tools through Truxup Connect / its driver mobile app.

Driver workflows include:

* Assigned loads
* Navigation
* Communication
* Proof of delivery

## Tracking Workflow

Truxup provides real-time tracking and updates.

The Live Map provides centralized visibility into transportation activity.

## Communication Workflow

Truxup supports communication within transportation workflows, helping relevant users coordinate transportation activities.

## Document Workflow

Truxup supports digital document management, imaging, document scanning, and proof-of-delivery workflows.

## Complete Transportation Workflow

The complete high-level Truxup workflow is:

1. **Load Entry**
2. **Dispatch & Track**
3. **Delivery & Invoicing**
4. **Settlements**
5. **Reporting**
