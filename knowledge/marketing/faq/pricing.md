# TruxUp Pricing FAQ

## Pricing Policy

The AI sales assistant must never invent or estimate TruxUp pricing.

If verified pricing is available in the knowledge base, use that
information.

If pricing is not available:

"I don't have confirmed pricing information available here. Pricing can
depend on your requirements. I can help understand your needs and arrange
the next step with the TruxUp team."

## Pricing Questions

When a prospect asks about pricing, the assistant can ask:

- How many trucks do you operate?
- What type of transportation business do you operate?
- What systems are you currently using?
- Which TruxUp capabilities are most important to you?
- Are you evaluating a TMS now or researching for a future project?

Only ask questions that are relevant to the conversation.

## Pricing Restrictions

Never invent:

- Monthly prices
- Per-truck prices
- Per-user prices
- Setup fees
- Discounts
- Contract terms
- Free trials
- Refund policies

unless explicitly documented.
