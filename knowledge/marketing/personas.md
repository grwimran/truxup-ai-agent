# TruxUp Customer Personas

## Purpose

This document describes the primary customer personas for TruxUp sales
and marketing conversations.

The AI sales assistant should use personas to understand the prospect's
business context and tailor the conversation.

Personas should guide discovery and messaging.

They must not be used to make unsupported assumptions about an individual
customer.

---

# Persona 1: Trucking Company Owner

## Profile

A trucking company owner or executive responsible for managing a
transportation business.

The company may operate a small, medium, or larger fleet.

## Typical Responsibilities

The owner may be responsible for:

- Fleet growth
- Customer relationships
- Transportation workflows
- Dispatch
- Driver management
- Financial performance
- Technology decisions
- Selecting or replacing a TMS

## Common Challenges

Potential challenges may include:

- Managing transportation workflows across multiple systems
- Heavy reliance on spreadsheets
- Manual dispatch processes
- Limited visibility
- Difficulty scaling processes as the fleet grows
- Administrative workload
- Fragmented information
- Difficulty obtaining useful business reports

These are potential challenges, not assumptions.

The assistant should confirm the actual pain point through discovery.

## What They May Care About

Possible priorities:

- Business visibility
- Ease of use
- Centralized workflows
- Scalability
- Operational organization
- Reporting
- Cost and value
- Technology consolidation

## Useful Discovery Questions

Examples:

- How are you currently managing your transportation workflow?
- What TMS are you using today?
- How large is your fleet?
- What part of your current process creates the most manual work?
- Are you planning to grow the fleet?
- What would you most like to improve in your current system?

## Messaging Approach

Focus on business outcomes and workflow improvement.

Example:

"If you're managing several transportation processes across different
tools, it may be useful to look at how TruxUp could centralize those
workflows."

# Persona Matching Rules

The AI assistant should not immediately assume a persona.

Instead, identify the persona from information provided by the visitor.

For example:

Customer:
"We operate 75 trucks and are looking for a new TMS."

Possible persona:
Trucking Company Owner / Executive

Customer:
"I'm a dispatcher and I'm spending most of my day updating
spreadsheets."

Possible persona:
Dispatcher

Customer:
"We broker freight and need better visibility into carrier loads."

Possible persona:
Freight Broker

# Important AI Rules

Personas are contextual guidance.

The assistant must never:

1. Assume the customer's role without evidence.
2. Assume the customer's company size.
3. Assume their current TMS.
4. Assume their pain point.
5. Assume buying intent.
6. Claim that a particular feature solves a problem unless supported by
   product knowledge.
7. Invent ROI or financial outcomes.
8. Invent pricing.
9. Invent integrations.
10. Pressure a prospect into requesting a demo.

The assistant should discover information naturally through conversation.

# Persona and Lead Profile

Information discovered during the conversation may contribute to the
lead profile:

- companyName
- customerType
- companySize
- currentTms
- painPoint
- buyingIntent
- demoRequested

Only populate a field when the customer provides enough information to
support it.

Unknown information should remain null or unknown rather than being
guessed.
