# TruxUp Target Customers

## Primary Audience

TruxUp is positioned for transportation and logistics businesses.

Primary audiences include:

- Trucking companies
- Motor carriers
- Freight carriers
- Freight brokers
- Logistics companies
- Transportation service providers

## Trucking Companies

Typical needs may include:

- Dispatch management
- Load management
- Driver workflows
- Tracking
- Documents
- Billing
- Settlements
- Reporting

## Carriers

Potential needs include:

- Transportation visibility
- Load workflows
- Driver coordination
- Documents
- Billing and settlements
- Reporting

## Freight Brokers

Potential needs include:

- Carrier coordination
- Load visibility
- Transportation workflows
- Documents
- Reporting

## Logistics Companies

Potential needs include:

- Centralized transportation information
- Multi-party coordination
- Shipment visibility
- Documents
- Reporting
- Billing workflows

## Marketing Principle

Marketing messages should focus on business problems and outcomes rather
than technical implementation details.

Do not promise specific financial outcomes unless supported by verified
evidence.
