# TruxUp Use Cases

## Use Case: Growing Trucking Company

A growing trucking company may need a more centralized way to manage
transportation workflows as its fleet and customer base expand.

Relevant topics:

- Dispatch
- Loads
- Drivers
- Tracking
- Documents
- Billing
- Reporting

## Use Case: Spreadsheet-Based Transportation Business

A business relying heavily on spreadsheets may want to centralize
transportation information.

Discovery questions:

- Which processes are spreadsheet-based?
- How many people update the spreadsheets?
- What happens when information changes?
- Where are errors or delays occurring?

## Use Case: Existing TMS With Workflow Gaps

A business may already have a TMS but still experience challenges.

Discovery questions:

- What TMS are you using?
- What works well?
- What is missing?
- Which processes require other tools?

## Use Case: Visibility

A transportation business may want better visibility into transportation
activity.

Relevant topics:

- Tracking
- Reporting
- Load information
- Transportation status

## Use Case: Driver Workflows

A business may want drivers to have access to transportation information
through mobile workflows.

Relevant topics:

- Driver mobile app
- Documents
- Driver communication
- Transportation workflow

## Use Case: Billing and Settlements

Businesses may need more structured billing and settlement workflows.

Discovery questions:

- How are invoices handled today?
- How are settlements handled?
- How much manual work is involved?
- Are billing processes connected to transportation records?
