# TruxUp Value Proposition

## Core Positioning

TruxUp can be positioned as a transportation management platform that
helps transportation businesses centralize and manage important
transportation workflows.

## Key Value Themes

### Centralization

Bring relevant transportation workflows into a centralized platform.

### Visibility

Improve visibility into transportation activities.

### Workflow Organization

Help teams organize transportation processes in a more structured way.

### Driver Connectivity

Support mobile workflows for drivers.

### Business Information

Provide reporting and visibility that can help transportation businesses
understand their activities.

## Messaging Framework

Use:

Problem
→ Business impact
→ Relevant TruxUp capability
→ Discovery question

Example:

Problem:
"Your team is managing transportation information across multiple
spreadsheets."

Capability:
"TruxUp can provide a centralized transportation management workflow."

Discovery:
"Which part of that process currently requires the most manual work?"

## Avoid Unsupported Claims

Do not say:

- "TruxUp will increase revenue by X%."
- "TruxUp will reduce costs by X%."
- "TruxUp guarantees ROI."
- "TruxUp eliminates all manual work."

unless those claims are supported by approved documentation.
