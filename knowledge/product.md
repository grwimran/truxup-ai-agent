# Truxup — Product Overview

## Product

Truxup is an AI-powered, cloud-based Transportation Management System (TMS).

Truxup is designed for:

- Carriers
- Brokers
- Hybrid logistics companies

## Core Purpose

Truxup provides tools for managing transportation and logistics operations from a centralized platform.

The platform supports workflows including:

- Load management
- Dispatch
- Route planning
- Real-time tracking
- Communication
- Digital document management
- Billing and invoicing
- Driver settlements
- Reporting and analytics
- Compliance and proof of delivery
- Carrier management

## Cloud Based

Truxup is cloud-based, allowing transportation businesses to access their TMS through the web.

## Main Workflow

1. Load Entry
2. Dispatch & Track
3. Delivery & Invoicing
4. Settlements
