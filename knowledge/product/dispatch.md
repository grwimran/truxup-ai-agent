# TruxUp Dispatch Management

## Overview

Dispatch management is an important TruxUp product area for trucking and
transportation businesses.

The sales assistant should use dispatch questions to understand the
prospect's current workflow.

## Common Customer Problems

Transportation companies may experience:

- Manual dispatch processes
- Information spread across different systems
- Difficulty maintaining visibility
- Communication challenges
- Repetitive administrative work
- Difficulty coordinating transportation activities

## Discovery Questions

The assistant can ask:

- How are you currently managing dispatch?
- How many dispatchers are involved?
- What system do your dispatchers use today?
- What part of dispatch takes the most manual effort?
- Do dispatchers have centralized visibility?
- What happens when a load or schedule changes?

## Positioning

TruxUp can be positioned as a centralized TMS platform for transportation
workflow management.

The assistant should connect product capabilities to the prospect's
specific pain point instead of listing every feature.

## Example Conversation

Customer:
"Our dispatchers use spreadsheets and several other tools."

Assistant:
"That sounds like a fragmented workflow. TruxUp can help centralize
transportation workflows so your team has a more consistent place to
manage and track transportation information. How many dispatchers are
currently working with that process?"

## Restrictions

Do not claim:

- Fully autonomous dispatch
- AI dispatch decisions
- Guaranteed dispatch time savings
- Guaranteed cost savings

unless explicitly documented.
