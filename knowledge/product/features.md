# TruxUp Features

## Feature Categories

TruxUp's sales conversations can cover the following product areas:

- Load management
- Dispatch management
- Route optimization
- Tracking
- Driver mobile workflows
- Document management
- Billing
- Settlements
- Reporting
- Carrier workflows
- Broker workflows

## Load Management

TruxUp can be positioned as a centralized system for managing
transportation-related load information.

Relevant sales discussion areas include:

- Organizing load information
- Maintaining transportation records
- Connecting load-related workflows
- Improving visibility into transportation activities

The AI assistant must not claim specific automated load features unless
they are documented in the product knowledge base.

## Dispatch Management

Dispatch-related capabilities can be discussed with transportation
companies that need to coordinate loads, drivers, vehicles, and
transportation activities.

Potential business outcomes include:

- Better organization
- Centralized information
- Improved visibility
- Reduced dependence on disconnected workflows

Do not claim that TruxUp automatically performs dispatch decisions unless
that capability is explicitly documented.

## Route Optimization

Route optimization is a TruxUp product capability that can be discussed
with prospects interested in improving transportation planning.

Relevant discovery questions include:

- How are routes currently planned?
- Are dispatchers manually planning routes?
- Do planners have difficulty managing changing transportation
  requirements?
- Is route visibility important to the business?

## Tracking

Tracking capabilities can be discussed with prospects that need greater
visibility into transportation activity.

Relevant customer needs include:

- Shipment visibility
- Vehicle visibility
- Customer visibility
- Transportation status visibility

Do not claim a specific tracking technology, update frequency, or
accuracy level unless documented.

## Driver Mobile App

TruxUp includes a driver-oriented mobile workflow.

Potential discussion areas include:

- Driver communication
- Driver workflow
- Transportation information
- Documents
- Mobile access

The assistant should only describe specific mobile functionality when it
is documented.

## Documents

Document-related workflows can be discussed as part of transportation
management.

Relevant business needs include:

- Organizing transportation documents
- Connecting documents with transportation workflows
- Improving document visibility
- Reducing manual document handling

## Billing

Billing is one of the product areas that can be discussed with
transportation businesses.

Discovery questions can include:

- How is billing currently handled?
- Is billing information spread across multiple systems?
- How much manual work is involved?
- Does the business need better visibility into billing workflows?

## Settlements

Settlements can be discussed with businesses that manage carrier or
driver payment workflows.

The assistant should not claim specific settlement calculations,
payment schedules, or accounting integrations unless documented.

## Reporting

Reporting can be discussed with prospects that need greater visibility
into transportation business activity.

Potential reporting topics include:

- Transportation activity
- Load information
- Business performance
- Operational visibility

Do not invent specific reports, dashboards, KPIs, or analytics capabilities.
