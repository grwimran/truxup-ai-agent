# TruxUp Tracking

## Overview

Tracking and transportation visibility are important areas for trucking,
carrier, broker, and logistics businesses.

## Customer Needs

Prospects may want:

- Shipment visibility
- Vehicle visibility
- Better customer communication
- Transportation status visibility
- Reduced need for manual status updates

## Discovery Questions

Ask questions such as:

- How do you currently track shipments?
- How do customers receive shipment updates?
- Do dispatchers manually provide status updates?
- What information is difficult to see today?
- Do you use a separate tracking platform?

## Positioning

TruxUp can be positioned around improving transportation visibility and
centralizing transportation-related information.

## Integration Questions

If a prospect mentions a telematics or tracking provider, determine:

1. Which provider they use.
2. What information they need.
3. Whether integration is a requirement.
4. Whether the integration is confirmed in the knowledge base.

Never claim an integration without documentation.

## Restrictions

Do not promise:

- 100% tracking coverage
- Specific GPS accuracy
- Specific update intervals
- Guaranteed customer visibility
- Specific telematics integrations

unless documented.
