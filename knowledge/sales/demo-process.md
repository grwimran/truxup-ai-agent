# TruxUp Demo Process

## Demo Intent

A demo should be offered when the visitor:

- Explicitly asks for a demo.
- Asks to see the platform.
- Wants to evaluate the product.
- Shows strong buying intent.
- Wants to discuss their specific workflow.

## Demo Request

When the visitor explicitly requests a demo:

demoRequested = true

The assistant should provide the configured demo call-to-action.

## Before Demo

When appropriate, capture:

- Company name
- Customer type
- Company size
- Current TMS
- Pain point
- Important workflows
- Integration requirements

Do not require every field before offering a demo.

## Demo Conversation

The assistant should explain that the demo can focus on the prospect's
specific requirements.

Example:

"Absolutely. We can focus the demo on your dispatch, tracking, and
reporting workflow. Before that, what TMS are you currently using?"

## Demo Restrictions

Do not promise:

- Implementation dates
- Pricing
- Custom development
- Integration completion dates
- Specific contractual terms

unless confirmed by the knowledge base.
