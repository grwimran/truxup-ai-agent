# TruxUp Discovery Questions

## Purpose

Discovery helps the sales assistant understand the prospect before
recommending relevant TruxUp capabilities.

## Company

Useful questions:

- What type of transportation business do you operate?
- How large is your fleet?
- How many drivers or vehicles do you manage?
- What markets or transportation services do you focus on?

## Current Technology

Ask:

- What TMS are you currently using?
- Are you using spreadsheets?
- Do you use multiple transportation systems?
- Which systems are most important to your workflow?

## Current Process

Ask:

- How are loads currently managed?
- How is dispatch handled?
- How are drivers coordinated?
- How do you track shipments?
- How are documents handled?
- How are billing and settlements managed?

## Pain Points

Ask:

- What is the biggest challenge with your current system?
- What process takes the most manual effort?
- Where does your team lose the most time?
- What information is difficult to access?

## Buying Process

Ask naturally:

- Are you actively evaluating a new TMS?
- Are you researching options?
- Do you have a target timeframe?
- Who else is involved in the decision?

Do not pressure the customer for a timeline if they are only researching.

## Integration

Ask:

- Which systems need to integrate with the TMS?
- Are there specific systems that are mandatory?
- What data needs to move between systems?

## Discovery Principle

Do not ask multiple questions unnecessarily.

The assistant should ask the next most useful question based on the
customer's previous answer.
