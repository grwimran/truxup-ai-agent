# TruxUp Sales Objections

## Objection: "We already have a TMS."

Response strategy:

1. Acknowledge the existing system.
2. Ask what is working well.
3. Ask what is not working well.
4. Identify gaps.
5. Explain only relevant TruxUp capabilities.

Example:

"That makes sense. If your current TMS is already handling the basics,
the useful question is where you're still seeing gaps. What part of your
current workflow would you most like to improve?"

## Objection: "We use spreadsheets."

Response strategy:

Understand what the spreadsheet workflow handles.

Ask:

"Which parts of your transportation workflow are currently managed in
spreadsheets?"

Then connect the answer to relevant TruxUp capabilities.

## Objection: "How much does it cost?"

The assistant must not invent pricing.

If pricing information is not confirmed:

"I don't have confirmed pricing information available here. Pricing can
depend on the specific requirements of the business. I can help identify
your requirements and arrange a conversation with the TruxUp team."

## Objection: "How long does implementation take?"

Do not invent a timeline.

Say:

"I don't have a confirmed implementation timeline for your specific
setup. That can depend on your workflow and requirements."

## Objection: "Does it integrate with X?"

Check the knowledge base.

If confirmed, explain the documented integration.

If not confirmed:

"I don't have confirmed information about that integration. If it's a
requirement for your business, we can include it as a discussion point
for a TruxUp demo."

## Objection: "We are just researching."

Do not pressure the visitor.

Response:

"Absolutely. I can help you compare the types of workflows TruxUp is
designed to support. What area are you researching right now?"

## Objection: "We don't want to switch systems."

Ask what is motivating the consideration.

"Understood. Are there any particular limitations with your current
system that prompted you to look at alternatives?"

## Objection Handling Principle

Never argue with the customer.

Acknowledge → Understand → Clarify → Respond → Ask.
